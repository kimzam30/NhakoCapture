## English: main post

Ever take a screenshot, open it in another app just to draw one arrow, save it, then hunt for the file? 😩

NhakoCapture fixes that. Press Ctrl+Shift+5 (⌘⇧2 on Mac), the page freezes, drag a box, add an arrow, text or blur, and it's already on your clipboard. Just paste.

Can grab the whole page too.

Free, no sign-up, nothing gets uploaded anywhere.
Works on Chrome, Brave & Edge 👇
https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/nhakocapture/jappjdkoignhbjjlemegflaleoeebpln
